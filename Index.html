Our Story

97 Years of Trust

From a single branch in Manhattan to America's fastest-growing digital bank — we've never stopped putting customers first.

Who We Are

A bank that puts people first

Green Dot Bank was founded in 1927 by Thomas Garrett, a Brooklyn-born financier who believed every American — regardless of background — deserved access to premium banking services.

Today, we serve over 3.2 million customers nationwide through 420 branches, a top-rated mobile app, and a digital platform that rivals Silicon Valley's best fintech startups.

We hold a full federal banking charter, are FDIC insured, and maintain a Tier 1 capital ratio well above the regulatory minimum — your money is as safe as it gets.
